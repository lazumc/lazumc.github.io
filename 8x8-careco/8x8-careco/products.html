📅 1 July 2026 ✍️ CareCo Press Team Product News

CareCo's Big Summer Sale 2026: hundreds of products reduced, next-day delivery included

CareCo has launched its biggest sale of the year, slashing prices across mobility scooters, powerchairs, walking aids, fireside chairs and bathroom equipment. With over 500 products available across 8 UK showrooms and delivered directly to customers' doors, this summer's sale represents an extraordinary opportunity for anyone seeking to maintain their independence at home.

Chief among the offers is an extended range of lightweight travel scooters, now available with a Price Match Promise and same-week delivery. Customers can visit any showroom — including the newly opened Edinburgh location — to try products before purchasing, with no pressure and no time limit on their visit.

Next-day delivery, 7 days a week

One of CareCo's most celebrated innovations this year is the extension of its next-day delivery service to all seven days of the week, including Sundays. For customers who rely on their equipment urgently, this commitment removes a barrier that no other UK mobility retailer has fully addressed. Orders placed before 3pm are dispatched the same day with live tracking from door to doorstep.

We understand that mobility isn't a luxury — it's a necessity. When a customer orders a scooter or a walking aid, every day they wait matters. Seven-day delivery is our answer to that.

— CareCo Operations Director, Summer 2026 press briefing

Price Match Promise: no customer pays more than they should

CareCo's Price Match Promise guarantees that if a customer finds the same product cheaper elsewhere, CareCo will match the price without question. Combined with VAT relief for eligible customers and interest-free finance options, CareCo's range is genuinely accessible to a broader audience than at any previous point in the company's history.

📅 15 March 2026 ✍️ Sarah Mitchell Award

Which? Trusted Trader: why CareCo earned the UK's most respected consumer badge — again

For the third year in a row, CareCo has been recognised as a Which? Trusted Trader — the independent consumer organisation's mark of excellence awarded only to businesses that demonstrate consistent quality, transparent pricing and genuine customer care. Of the thousands of UK retailers assessed, fewer than 2% achieve this status.

The accreditation process involved mystery shopping visits to CareCo showrooms, detailed analysis of customer review data across independent platforms, and a review of complaint-handling procedures. In each category, CareCo scored above the sector average for home mobility retailers.

What it means for CareCo customers

The Which? Trusted Trader badge is a living commitment: businesses that fail to maintain standards lose the accreditation. For CareCo customers, it is an assurance that the prices they see are the prices they pay, that products are as described, and that after-sales support is available should anything go wrong.

CareCo stood out not just for the range they offer, but for how they make customers feel. Their showroom staff are trained to listen first and sell second — that's rare, and it shows in the feedback we received.

— Which? Trusted Trader assessment report, Spring 2026
