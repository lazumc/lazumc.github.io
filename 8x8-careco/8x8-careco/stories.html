📅 18 May 2026 ✍️ CareCo Editorial Team Customer Story

"The staff let me try every single chair until I found the right one" — Karen's story

When Karen received her diagnosis of motor neurone disease in early 2025, she was told that everyday activities she had taken for granted — sitting comfortably, rising from a chair unaided, spending evenings in the lounge with her family — would become increasingly difficult. Her consultant suggested she look into specialist seating before her symptoms progressed. A friend recommended CareCo.

Karen visited the Brierley Hill showroom on a Tuesday morning, expecting a quick transaction. What she found instead was three hours of unhurried expert guidance. "The staff member who helped me was absolutely lovely," she recalls. "She never once rushed me. I must have tried fifteen different chairs. She explained every single one — the rise angle, the fabric options, the heat and massage functions. She genuinely wanted me to leave with the right chair, not the most expensive one."

Finding the perfect fit — the Coniston Velvet Teal

After extensive testing, Karen settled on the Coniston Velvet Teal fireside chair — a model she hadn't even considered when she walked in. "It's perfect for me. I can rise from it without needing help, which means I can still get up to make a cup of tea independently. That sounds small but it means everything." Her family has since purchased a rollator walker and bathroom grab rail kit from the same showroom.

CareCo staff was lovely and they let me try all the chairs, just to make sure I got the right one for me. I didn't feel rushed or pressured for even a second. I left with exactly what I needed.

— Karen, diagnosed with MND in 2025, Brierley Hill showroom customer

CareCo's try-before-you-buy philosophy

Karen's experience reflects a company-wide commitment that sets CareCo apart from online-only mobility retailers. Every showroom is staffed by trained mobility advisors — not sales assistants — who are encouraged to spend as much time as a customer needs. There is no pressure to purchase and no commission-based sales structure. The priority is always the right product for the right person.

Why CareCo's Trustpilot score is the highest in UK mobility retail — and what keeps it there

A 4.8-star Trustpilot rating, sustained across thousands of reviews and multiple years, is not an accident. It is the result of a consistent operational culture: hire people who care, train them properly, stock products that genuinely work, and stand behind every sale with meaningful after-sales support.

The most common phrases in CareCo's five-star reviews are telling: "no pressure," "took their time," "the product changed my life," and "I wish I had come sooner." These are responses to a company that treats every customer as an individual with specific needs, not a transaction to be processed.

The showroom difference

In an era dominated by online retail, CareCo's investment in physical showrooms continues to pay off. Customers who visit in person report higher satisfaction than those who order online — not because the online experience is poor, but because trying products before purchasing creates a confidence that translates directly into long-term satisfaction.
